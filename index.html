Dark Patterns in Retail Trading Apps — Behavioral Finance Analysis

Mixed-methods MSc dissertation (supervised by Dr. Ashlesha Khedekar) examining the prevalence and behavioural effects of dark patterns in Indian retail trading platforms (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One, Paytm Money) on retail investor decision-making.

  • Systematic content analysis across 5 platforms using Mathur et al. (2019) taxonomy — 8 dark pattern categories mapped across user journeys
  • Between-subjects experimental survey on 108 participants; Treatment vs Control group across 7 task-based scenarios
  • Urgency (p=0.002, r=0.295) and Sneaking (p=0.021, r=0.222) showed statistically significant psychological effects
  • Key finding: Awareness ≠ Resistance (Spearman ρ = -0.002, p = 0.986) — disclosure-based regulation insufficient
  • DPEI (cumulative psychological burden) significantly higher in treatment group (p=0.040)
